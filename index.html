Our story

A neighborhood barbershop, done right.

Dominion Barber Shop has been McLean's go-to for classic men's grooming for over twenty years. Our skilled barbers bring old-school craft to every chair — sharp lines, clean fades, and a straight-razor finish, delivered with the kind of consistency that turns first-timers into twenty-year regulars.

It's more than a haircut. Settle in for a hot-towel finish and our signature head, neck & shoulder massage — a small ritual that leaves you looking sharp and feeling relaxed. Prompt, friendly, and rarely a wait, even on a busy Saturday.

  • Skilled barbers, consistent results
  • Straight-razor detailing & hot towel
  • Signature head & shoulder massage
  • Fast, friendly — hardly ever a wait

What we do

Services & craft

Traditional barbering with a personal touch. Walk-ins are welcome — ask for your favorite barber, or let us match you with the right one.

Signature Haircut

A precise, tailored men's cut finished with straight-razor edging and a hot-towel touch — our classic, start to finish.

Straight-Razor Neck & Line

Crisp, clean lines around the neck and edges with a fresh, opened-in-front-of-you straight-razor blade.

Beard Trim & Shape

Shape, trim, and detail your beard for a sharp, well-groomed finish that complements your cut.

Traditional Shave

Hot towel, rich lather, and a close, comfortable straight-razor shave in the timeless barbershop tradition.

Head, Neck & Shoulder Massage

Our signature finishing touch — a therapeutic massage many clients call the best part of the visit.

Children's Haircut

Patient, friendly cuts for the little ones — bring the whole family to the neighborhood shop.
