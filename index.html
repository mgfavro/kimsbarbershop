Our story

A neighborhood barbershop, done right.

Kim's Family Barber was built on a simple idea: everyone deserves a great haircut and a moment to unwind. With over fifteen years behind the chair, Kim brings the classic barbering tradition to McLean — sharp lines, careful attention to detail, and a warm welcome for every client.

From a first-time visit to a decades-long routine, regulars describe the shop as a place where "you feel like you're at home." Clean, calm, and unhurried — finished with a neck massage and a look you'll be proud of.

  • Meticulous, detail-first cuts
  • Traditional shave & grooming services
  • Family-friendly — kids & seniors welcome
  • Relaxed, spotless environment
